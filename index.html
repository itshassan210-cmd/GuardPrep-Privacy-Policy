Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the GuardPrep Android application handles information. GuardPrep is an offline study and examination preparation app for Security Operations Level 3.

1. Information We Collect

GuardPrep does not require account registration. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to a GuardPrep server.

The app stores information locally on your device to provide its features. This may include:

  • Quiz answers, scores and attempt history.
  • Incorrect-question records and study progress.
  • App settings and preferences.
  • Question banks and content that you import.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored information supports quizzes, progress tracking, settings and question-bank management. The app processes pasted PDF text and JSON question-bank content on your device.

GuardPrep does not upload this pasted content or your quiz progress to GuardPrep servers.

3. Internet Access and Third Parties

The compiled GuardPrep APK does not request the Android INTERNET permission. The app's current features operate offline. It does not include app-side advertising, analytics or account services.

Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may process information under their own services and policies. Those services are outside the app's control.

4. Clipboard and Export

If you choose the question-bank export or copy feature, GuardPrep places the exported JSON text on your device clipboard. You control whether and where you paste or share that content. Clipboard handling is also subject to Android system behaviour.

5. Device Storage and Backup

Quiz information, settings and imported content are stored locally in the app's private storage. GuardPrep disables Android app-data backup through its application configuration.

You can remove locally stored app information by clearing GuardPrep's app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Exported or copied content may remain wherever you chose to save or share it.

6. Permissions

The compiled APK requests the VIBRATE permission for tactile feedback. It also declares an app-specific AndroidX permission for internal, non-exported broadcast receiver protection. GuardPrep does not request internet, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

GuardPrep is a general educational study tool and is not designed specifically to collect information from children. The app does not provide account registration or app-side personal-data collection.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
